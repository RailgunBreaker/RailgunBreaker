## About Me

I am an incoming **Master of Arts in Political Science student at Waseda University**, where I plan to study international political economy, trade reorganization, and semiconductor supply-chain resilience under the supervision of **Prof. Shuhei Kurizaki**.

Before Waseda, I completed a dual bachelor's degree in **Global Liberal Arts at Ritsumeikan University** and **Asia Pacific Affairs at the Australian National University**. ## Featured Project

### [SSCIM — Semiconductor Supply Chain Intelligence Map](https://sscim.railgunbreaker.icu)

SSCIM is an interactive research prototype for exploring concentration, dependencies, and shock propagation across the global semiconductor supply chain.

- Models **24 production and end-market stages**, **16 countries**, and **109 companies**
- Connects geography, industry structure, company relationships, policy events, and hypothetical disruption scenarios
- Uses transparent network-based propagation logic to distinguish structural vulnerability, current modeled impact, and scenario change
- Designed for investors, policymakers, researchers, and students studying semiconductor resilience and geopolitical risk

**Technologies:** React, TypeScript, Vite, JavaScript, data visualization, network analysis, GitHub Pages
